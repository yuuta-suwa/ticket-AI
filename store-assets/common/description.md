# Description (English) — plain, no guarantees claimed
**Know when to buy a flight — and when to wait.**
DEAL HUNTER AI tracks flight price history, spots prices that are far from normal, and gives a clear BUY / WAIT / WATCH recommendation with the reasons.

• EXTREME DEAL detection: see when a price is far below its usual range
• BUY / WAIT / WATCH with confidence and plain-language reasons
• Price history charts and (Premium) forecast ranges
• Watchlist + alerts: choose deal types, routes, minimum score, price thresholds and quiet hours
• Latest-price check before you go to the seller — we re-verify first
• Works with cached data when you're offline

FREE: basic search & analysis, limited watchlist, standard alerts, ads. PREMIUM (subscription): Advanced Forecast, instant EXTREME DEAL alerts, expanded watchlist, detailed charts, priority monitoring, no ads.

DEAL HUNTER AI is a decision-support tool. We don't sell or issue tickets; you book with the airline or a booking partner. Prices change in real time and forecasts are estimates, not guarantees.
Subscriptions renew automatically unless cancelled ≥24h before period end; manage in your App Store / Google Play account. Terms and Privacy: see links in the app.
